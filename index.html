Focus-then-Context: Subject-Centric Progressive Visual Token Reduction for VLMs

Yulin Zhao, Zheng Zhang

arXiv, 2026 (Under Review)

We propose SPpruner, a subject-centric progressive visual token reduction framework that preserves salient subjects and their contextual relationships, realizing up to 2.53× speedup on Qwen2.5-VL and a 67% FLOPs reduction on LLaVA with only a 0.6% accuracy drop.

Towards Effective and General Graph Unlearning via Mutual Evolution

Xunkai Li#, Yulin Zhao#, Zhengyu Wu, Wentao Zhang, Rong-Hua Li, Guoren Wang

AAAI 2024 (Oral Presentation)

We propose MEGU, a mutual-evolution graph unlearning framework that jointly improves predictive and unlearning capabilities, realizing average gains of 2.7%, 2.5%, and 3.2% on feature-, node-, and edge-unlearning while reducing time and space costs by 159.8× and 9.6× versus retraining.
